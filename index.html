Suggested study routine

  1. Read the chapter in your Exam Focus textbook.
  2. Write down key definitions, principles and formulae.
  3. Work through the examples in the textbook.
  4. Attempt the revision questions without checking the answers first.
  5. Review mistakes, then mark this chapter complete.
